## Sanity filters applied at routing time

- Drop models with `beta_access` (`model_spec.beta`) unless your key has beta access. `beta_status` (`model_spec.betaModel`) models are callable but may change or disappear; prefer non-beta when tied.
- Drop models with `model_spec.offline === true`.
